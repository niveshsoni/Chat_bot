This playbook is for internal use only and its purpose is to help you appropriately utilize approved ISA content for both brands, ensuring a fair and balanced conversation with your HCPs.

GUIDING PRINCIPLES
• When discussing RINVOQ or SKYRIZI as a recommended treatment option for patients with moderate to severe Crohn's disease, it is important to reference the FULL RINVOQ or SKYRIZI indication. Any claims about RINVOQ or SKYRIZI must be communicated in a balanced manner (e.g., full indication, including data context, safety risks, limitations of data, etc.) to appropriately and responsibly provide the HCPs.
  • Be sure to detail primary endpoints before continuing to detail on additional RINVOQ or SKYRIZI data (i.e., ranked secondary endpoints, open-label extension data, H2H, post hoc analysis, additional endpoints, CTs, etc.). Clarify the indicated patient population you are referring to in your discussion and support your discussion with relevant study design information
• Unsolicited requests for off-label information must be referred to AbbVie Global Medical Information (1-800-633-9110) in accordance with AbbVie policies and procedures
• Appropriate MSL and Sales Representative Collaboration when an HCP requests MSL interaction:
  • Submit to GMI: If an HCP asks a sales representative for assistance in getting a meeting set up with a Medical Science Liaison (MSL) and the HCP has medical information questions, the sales representative must direct both requests to Global Medical Information (GMI)
  • Contact MSL: If an HCP asks a sales representative for assistance in getting a meeting set up with an MSL and there is NO medical information request, the sales representative may contact the MSL directly and notify them of the HCP's request

PORTFOLIO GUIDELINES
• Critical to open every call with the portfolio overview by setting up the objectives of discussing the two product types for each brand
• For each brand, integrate mini agreements and create active commitments in response to a question
• Close for each brand within the call
• Clear transition statements must be used before transitioning to a branded conversation or using a branded resource in the same call
• Use "SKYRIZI or RINVOQ for Crohn's disease" or "SKYRIZI/RINVOQ for Crohn's" instead of "SRV CD" or "RNV CD"

CHALLENGER INSIGHTS OVERVIEW

Challengers teach by delivering commercial insights in a rational and emotionally compelling way in dialogue with customers. They use insights to help provide their customers with unique and valuable perspectives on the market.

INSIGHT
Deliver an insight in a rational and emotionally compelling way to create a two-way dialogue with your HCPs. An insight reframes preconceptions and starts conversations that lead to new ways of thinking. Only one insight is to be used per call.

POWERFUL QUESTIONS
Ask Powerful Questions to identify an HCP's concerns and help initiate the conversation around treatment considerations.

TRANSITION
Steer the conversation back to the task at hand. Use CHA (Clarify, Handle, Advance): How can SKYRIZI or RINVOQ help your appropriate patients?

You may not go back to unbranded content after you have transitioned to branded content, except in instances in which an HCP asks you an unsolicited question about the unbranded content. You may address the question, then transition it back to branded content.

"Just so you know, I am going back to the disease state awareness conversation we started with, but I want to be clear that I am not linking the two together. I just want to answer your question. Once I do that, we can then transition back to branded conversation."

CHALLENGER INSIGHTS GUARDRAILS

Challenger Insights are ONLY USED BEFORE transitioning to a branded call.

- See SKYRIZI and RINVOQ Challenger Insights ISA (see for full screen insights layout for discussion)
- You may not go back to unbranded content after you have transitioned to branded content, except in instances in which an HCP asks you an unsolicited question about the unbranded content. You may address the question, then transition back to branded content.
- "Just so you know, I am going back to the disease state awareness conversation we started with, but I wanted to be clear that I am not linking the two together. I just want to answer your question. Once I do that, we can then transition to branded content."
- To transition to a branded call, please preface with the fact that you are now going to discuss branded content
- You can use the guardrails of a Challenger Insight and proceed directly to the ISI and branded overview file
- Do not draw additional conclusions or insights to the insights presented other than what is provided in the Playbook
- When delivering Challenger Insights, ensure you cover background, situations, study designs, and implications in context

INSIGHT
In a retrospective claims analysis (2005-2013) of 4,569 moderately to severely active Crohn's patients,

UP TO 40% OF PATIENTS RECEIVING TNFis FOR ONE YEAR DISCONTINUED THERAPY, WHILE UP TO 24% EXPERIENCED DOSE ESCALATION.*

POWERFUL QUESTION
- What are some challenges that you and your patients face when you decide that dose escalation is needed?

TRANSITION
I'd like to show you why you should consider SKYRIZI as an option for appropriate bio-naïve patients.

*In the claims analysis, discontinuation was defined as a gap of at least 60 consecutive days between the last day of supply of the initial biologic therapy for CD (discontinuation date) and either the next prescription fill or the end of continuous healthcare plan enrollment or the end of data availability (ie, 12/31/2013), whichever occurred first.
In the claims analysis, dose escalation was defined as a dose increase during the maintenance phase, resulting in a dose twice as high as the recommended standard maintenance dose for CD.

LIMITATION: This retrospective claims analysis was conducted in the US using a database of commercially insured patients. Results may not be generalizable to patients who are enrolled in Medicare and Medicaid or have no or limited healthcare coverage. WHY DOES SKYRIZI HAVE A BOXED WARNING FOR HEPATOTOXICITY?

CLARIFY
Doctor, I appreciate you wanting to more fully understand the safety profile of SKYRIZI when making treatment decisions for your moderately to severely active Crohn's patients.

HANDLE
SKYRIZI does not have a boxed warning, but there is a warnings and precautions on hepatotoxicity in treatment of inflammatory bowel disease

REFER TO WELL-STUDIED SAFETY PROFILE (WEEK 12)
> HEALTH EVENTS POP-UP
A serious adverse reaction of drug-induced liver injury in conjunction with a rash that required hospitalization was reported in a patient with Crohn's disease (ALT 54x ULN, AST 30x ULN, and total bilirubin 2.2x ULN) following two 600 mg IV doses of SKYRIZI. The liver test abnormalities resolved following administration of steroids. SKYRIZI was subsequently discontinued.

Additionally, in treatment of inflammatory Bowel Disease, Drug-induced liver injury was reported in a patient with Crohn's disease who was hospitalized for a rash during induction dosing of SKYRIZI. For the treatment of Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis, evaluate liver enzymes and bilirubin at baseline and during induction (12 weeks), monitor thereafter according to routine patient management. Consider an alternate treatment for patients with evidence of liver cirrhosis. Interrupt treatment if drug-induced liver injury is suspected until this diagnosis is excluded. Instruct your patient to seek immediate medical attention if they experience symptoms suggestive of hepatic dysfunction

REFER TO DOSING > LAB MONITORING
- For the treatment of Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis, evaluate liver enzymes and bilirubin at baseline and during induction at least up to 12 weeks of treatment. Monitor thereafter according to routine patient management
- Consider other treatment options in patients with evidence of liver cirrhosis.
- Prompt investigation of the cause of liver enzyme elevation is recommended to identify potential cases of drug-induced liver injury. Interrupt treatment if drug-induced liver injury is suspected until this diagnosis is excluded. Instruct patients to seek immediate medical attention if they experience symptoms suggestive of hepatic dysfunction

  * The head-to-head study investigated the primary endpoints of clinical remission (CDAI <150) at Week 24 and endoscopic remission (SES-CD ≤4) at Week 24

* In SEQUENCE, the safety profile was generally consistent between the head-to-head trial and previously reported studies and the Prescribing Information
* SKYRIZI delivers symptom improvement (defined by clinical remission) and visible mucosal improvement (defined by endoscopic outcomes), and still offers you and your patients a well-studied safety profile

WHAT IS THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN AS OBSERVED (AO) AND NON-RESPONDER IMPUTATION (NRI)?

• SKYRIZI efficacy and safety from ADVANCE, MOTIVATE, and FORTIFY studies and RINVOQ's efficacy and safety outcomes from U-EXCEL, U-EXCEED, and MEASURE studies are randomized, controlled clinical trials that are based on non-responder imputation [NRI] incorporating multiple imputation [MI] (NRI-MI) analysis. This type of analysis considers patients with missing visit data as a treatment failure. The exception is if the reason for missing data was due to COVID-19 infection or logistical restriction due to the pandemic or geopolitical conflict, the patient's assessment was imputed using multiple imputation

• The SKYRIZI and RINVOQ open-label extension trial efficacy outcomes are presented as an as observed analysis. This type of analysis includes all available data for the analysis. Missing data is excluded from calculations for that visit, which may increase the percent of responders

• Missing visits may be due to several reasons including patient withdrawal, loss to follow-up, adverse event, or lack of efficacy

STUDY DESIGN

Why wasn't the study design a treat-through? Why wasn't there a true placebo arm carried into the maintenance phase?
In the study design of the SKYRIZI Phase 3 program aligns with clinical practice and FDA guidance. As in some other pivotal trials in advanced Crohn's disease therapies, patients who responded to active drug in the induction phase were the ones followed through in the maintenance phase.
* Note that there was a cohort of patients in the SKYRIZI program that followed the placebo group who continued on placebo in the maintenance phase; this "continuous placebo" arm was not intended for direct comparison and was not included in the primary efficacy analysis

What exactly does the term "continuous placebo" mean in relation to your clinical trials?
The continuous placebo patient cohort were those patients who received placebo in induction and were clinical responders (CR-100). These placebo patients were followed through maintenance but were not included in the primary efficacy analysis. Continuous placebo data are not intended for direct comparison.

Why were all patients in the open-label extension (OLE) given 180 mg?
* In the FORTIFY OLE trial, patients who received per label maintenance dosing (180 mg or 360 mg SC Q8W) during the 52-week maintenance period all received 180 mg SC Q8W
* The open-label extension trial study dosing protocol was based off the Phase 2 studies, which suggested that 180 mg SC Q8W was an efficacious and safe dose. The 360 mg SC Q8W dose had not been studied prior to the pivotal Phase 3 trials, and the efficacy and safety was unknown when the OLE study was designed

What are some of the most common reasons for why patients dropped out of the FORTIFY OLE?
Similar to all OLE studies, the most common reasons for study dropouts are loss of response and loss to follow-up over time.

Why was this head-to-head trial designed as an open-label trial?

• An open comparative head-to-head clinical trial like SEQUENCE is better opted for an open-label design. The cost, time, and recruitment challenges of blinding and adding a placebo arm for two drugs with two different dosing schedules would have significantly delayed the completion of the trial. In this open-label design, AbbVie, the site staff, and the patient all knew the treatment. To help account for bias, the CDAI calculation was blinded and the endoscopists were centrally read

• Please reach out to Global Medical Information at (1-800-633-9110) for more information

What are the implications/advantages of a treat-through study design?

• Treat-through designs are consistent with clinical practice where treatment is not interrupted after a fixed induction period with a potential change to placebo after response. This trial design also allows for an extended active comparator-controlled period, thereby allowing for assessment of response after the standard induction period

• In contrast to the re-randomized withdrawal designs in the SKYRIZI and Stelara clinical development programs, treat-through designs do not evaluate the durability of response after treatment withdrawal. As mentioned, though, this has been previously assessed for both products

• Cross-trial comparisons should be evaluated with caution, as relative efficacy can only be determined by well-designed head-to-head trials

What does "tested for multiplicity" mean?

Typically, studies are designed with many endpoints and comparisons, including different outcome measures, time points, and treatment comparisons (eg, drug 1 vs drug 2). With every test done, there is a risk or chance that the outcome may lead to a false positive. The more tests that are done, the higher the probability that a significant finding will be observed by chance. Adjusting or controlling for multiple tests (ie, multiplicity) is a statistical methodology used to reduce that risk, such that if an outcome is found to be positive, it has a lower risk of being a false positive finding. Outcomes of endpoints that have been adjusted or controlled for multiplicity are therefore considered more robust and have a higher level of credibility. The SEQUENCE trial contained several ranked multiplicity-controlled endpoints, including primary endpoints and ranked secondary endpoints.

What is the NRI-MI population?

The NRI-MI population is the nonresponder imputation for missing data. The exception is if the reason for missing data is due to COVID-19 infection or logistical restriction due to the pandemic or geopolitical conflict, the patient's assessment will be imputed using multiple imputation.

TALKING POINTS

• ADVANCE and FORTIFY are the Phase 3 studies supporting SKYRIZI's indication for across-indication approved trials. SKYRIZI studied 1419 patients across two 12-week induction trials, which were followed by one 52-week maintenance trial. An open-label extension is ongoing

• SKYRIZI's clinical program consisted of two 12-week induction studies with 58% advanced therapy failures and 42% conventional therapy failures (ADVANCE and 100% advanced therapy failures [half of patients failing more than one advanced therapy] in MOTIVATE). In the induction trials, patients were randomized to receive SKYRIZI 600 mg, risankizumab-rzaa 1,200 mg, or placebo as an intravenous infusion at Weeks 0, 4, and 8. The risankizumab-rzaa 1,200 mg dosage did not demonstrate additional treatment benefit over the 600 mg dosage and is not a recommended regimen

• Advanced therapy in Crohn's is defined as biologics

• At Week 12, patients who responded to SKYRIZI in the induction studies (52% of patients) were eligible to move into FORTIFY, the 52-week maintenance study. 27% of patients in FORTIFY were bio-naïve, while the remaining 73% of patients were advanced therapy failures. Patients were randomized and received either SKYRIZI 360 mg SC, SKYRIZI 180 mg, or placebo via subcutaneous injection every 8 weeks for 52 weeks

• The co-primary endpoints were endoscopic response (>50% decrease in SES-CD from baseline) and clinical remission (CDAI ≤150) at Week 12 for ADVANCE and MOTIVATE and at Week 52 for FORTIFY

• One additional patient cohort followed were patients that never received SKYRIZI before in the Continuous Placebo group. Both efficacy and safety in this group were assessed but are not meant for direct comparison
